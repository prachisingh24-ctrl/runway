# Runway

A consumer personal-finance app for Gen Z. Users get a ₹10,000, 30-day budget challenge and see how every choice — spending, subscriptions, savings goals, surprise costs — changes their balance, daily allowance, month-end forecast, risk level and Health Score.

The signature feature is the Decision Simulator: try a purchase or change before making it and see the before/after on every metric, then commit or discard.

Audience: young adults/students managing tight monthly budgets. Public app with email magic-link sign-in; each user only sees their own data.
